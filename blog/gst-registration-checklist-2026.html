GST Registration

GST Registration Checklist 2026: Do You Actually Need It, and What Documents Do You Need?

Updated September 2026 6 min read Reviewed by Onnmore's compliance team

GST registration confuses more small business owners than almost any other compliance step — mostly because "do I need it?" has more than one answer. Turnover matters, but it isn't the only trigger. This guide covers the 2026 thresholds, the situations where registration is mandatory regardless of turnover, and the exact documents you'll need to have ready.

Here's the short version: if your turnover crosses ₹40 lakh for goods or ₹20 lakh for services in most states, you must register. But certain businesses — e-commerce sellers, inter-state suppliers, casual taxable persons — must register regardless of turnover, sometimes from ₹1 of revenue. West Bengal falls under the standard (non-special-category) threshold.

The 2026 turnover thresholds

CategoryMost states (incl. West Bengal)Special category states
Exclusively supplying goods₹40 lakh₹20 lakh
Supplying services (or a mix)₹20 lakh₹10 lakh

Special category states include the North-Eastern states, Himachal Pradesh, and a few others with lower thresholds. Aggregate turnover is calculated on a PAN basis across all your business activities in India, not per business — and it includes taxable, exempt, and export supplies combined.

You must register regardless of turnover if you...

  • Sell through an e-commerce platform like Amazon, Flipkart, or your own online store with payment aggregation.
  • Operate an e-commerce platform yourself — every operator must register, no threshold exemption.
  • Make inter-state taxable supplies of goods — this applies from the first rupee, with a narrow exemption for small service providers under the threshold.
  • Are a casual taxable person or non-resident taxable person — occasional suppliers with no fixed place of business in the state.
  • Are required to deduct TDS or collect TCS under GST, or are liable to pay tax under the reverse charge mechanism.
  • Sell on behalf of another person, whether as an agent or principal.
  • Provide online services or online money gaming from outside India to unregistered individuals in India.
If none of the above applies to you and your turnover is below the threshold, GST registration is optional — but many small businesses register anyway to claim input tax credit and look more credible to larger clients who prefer dealing with GST-registered vendors.

The document checklist

Gather these before you start — it's the single biggest thing that speeds up approval:

  • PAN card of the business owner, partners, or the company (as applicable).
  • Aadhaar card of the proprietor, partners, or directors.
  • Photograph (passport-size) of the proprietor, partners, or directors.
  • Business address proof — a recent electricity bill, property tax receipt, or rent agreement with a no-objection certificate if the premises is rented.
  • Bank account details — a cancelled cheque, bank statement, or the first page of the passbook.
  • Business constitution proof — Certificate of Incorporation for a company/LLP, or the Partnership Deed for a partnership firm.
  • Digital Signature Certificate (DSC) — mandatory for companies and LLPs, optional for proprietorships.
  • Authorization letter naming an authorized signatory, where applicable.

What happens if you register late — or don't register at all

Operating above the threshold without registering isn't a minor paperwork lapse — it exposes you to penalties and can disrupt supply relationships, since GST-registered buyers generally won't transact with an unregistered supplier once the threshold is crossed. If your turnover exceeds the threshold even once during a financial year, registration becomes mandatory from that point, and it's worth applying promptly rather than waiting for a notice.

Our honest recommendation

Check both tests before assuming you're exempt: turnover and the mandatory-registration categories above. A freelancer well under ₹20 lakh who sells one project to a client in another state can still trigger inter-state registration rules. When in doubt, a quick check is faster and cheaper than sorting out a compliance gap later.

Frequently asked

Do I need GST registration if I'm under the turnover threshold?

Not necessarily — unless you fall into one of the mandatory categories (e-commerce, inter-state supply, casual taxable person, and others listed above), registration below the threshold is voluntary.

Can I cancel my GST registration if my turnover drops later?

Yes, using Form GST REG-16, though all pending returns must be filed and liabilities cleared before cancellation is approved.

Is the threshold different for West Bengal?

No — West Bengal follows the standard threshold (₹40 lakh for goods, ₹20 lakh for services), not the lower special-category limits.
