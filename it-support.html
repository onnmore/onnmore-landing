Do I need the WhatsApp Business API?

If you chat with a few customers from one phone, the free WhatsApp Business app in Online Starter is enough. Choose the API if you want automatic replies, lead alerts, broadcasts, or several team members on one number (Online Growth and Online Pro).

Is WhatsApp API approval guaranteed?

No. Meta verifies your business and approves each message template, and we cannot guarantee the result. We prepare your documents, submit templates and guide you through the process.
