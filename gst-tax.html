Who this is for

Registered businesses and individual taxpayers who need to register for GST, stay compliant with GST filings, or file their income tax returns.

When you may need this

When your turnover crosses the applicable GST threshold, when a marketplace or client requires a GSTIN before onboarding you, or simply when it's time to file your annual income tax return.

Typical documents

Exact requirements vary by case, but these are commonly needed:

  • PAN and Aadhaar of the proprietor/partners/directors
  • Business registration proof (if applicable)
  • Bank account details and a cancelled cheque
  • Address proof of the place of business
  • Digital Signature Certificate (for companies/LLPs)
  • Sales/purchase records for return filing

FAQs

Is GST registration compulsory for every business?

No — it depends on your turnover, business activity, location and the nature of your supplies. We'll check your specific case before recommending registration.

What happens if I miss a GST return deadline?

The GST department levies its own late fee and interest, which apply regardless of who files your return. We aim to file well before deadlines to avoid this.

Can I file my ITR myself instead of through Onnmore?

Of course — filing is always something you're free to do yourself. We're here if you'd like help getting it done accurately and on time.
