Is this invoice legally valid for GST purposes?

The format includes the common fields expected on a GST invoice (GSTIN, tax split, invoice number/date), but you should confirm any industry-specific requirements (like HSN/SAC codes) with your accountant before relying on it for filing.

Where is my data stored?

Nowhere but your browser tab — refreshing the page will clear it. Print or save as PDF to keep a copy.
