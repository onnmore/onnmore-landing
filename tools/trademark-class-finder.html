    Why trademark class matters

    Trademarks are registered against specific classes of goods or services (45 classes in total). Choosing the right class(es) determines what protection your mark actually gets — registering in the wrong class can leave your brand unprotected in the area that matters most.

    FAQs

    Can I register in more than one class?

    Yes — many businesses register in multiple classes if they offer a range of goods/services, though each class typically involves a separate application/fee.

    Does this tool check if my name is already trademarked?

    No — it only suggests which class(es) might be relevant to your goods/services. A separate trademark search is needed to check for conflicting existing marks.

    How long does trademark registration take?

    It varies significantly depending on objections/oppositions, but the process commonly takes many months to a couple of years from filing to registration.
