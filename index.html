Frequently Asked Questions

How long does company registration take?

Timelines depend on document readiness and government processing times. Once you share your requirement, our team will give you a realistic timeline for your specific case.

What documents will I need?

This varies by service — for most registrations you'll need basic ID and address proof, photographs, and business-related documents. We'll send you an exact checklist once you tell us which service you need.

Do I need to visit your office?

No. The entire process — from document sharing to filing — is handled online. You're welcome to visit our Newtown office if you'd prefer to meet in person.

How much will it cost?

Pricing depends on the service and your specific requirements. Share your details with us and we'll give you a clear, transparent quote before any work begins — no hidden charges.

Can you help with more than one service at once?

Yes. Many clients need multiple registrations together — for example Pvt Ltd registration along with GST and MSME. Just let us know everything you need and we'll put together a combined plan.

Use the "Get Free Consultation" form above, or message us directly on WhatsApp. Our team will respond with the next steps.
