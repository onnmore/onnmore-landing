Service Delivery & Fulfilment Policy

Nature of Services

Onnmore Technologies Private Limited is a private-sector business services and professional assistance company. Depending on the service selected, fulfilment may include business registration assistance, startup and business paperwork, document review, application preparation, filing assistance, licensing support, GST-related assistance, compliance support, process coordination, status communication and delivery of completed work product.

After Payment

After payment is received and the information and documents reasonably required for the selected service are available, Onnmore will commence the applicable work according to the agreed service scope. The exact service inclusions, professional fee and any separately applicable government, statutory or third-party charges are communicated to the customer before the relevant work begins.

Timelines

Service timelines depend on the selected scope, the customer's timely submission of complete and accurate information and documents, and the processing times of government authorities, regulators, banks or other third parties where applicable. Onnmore does not guarantee a fixed government processing time, approval date or statutory outcome unless expressly stated in writing for a specific service.

Digital Delivery & Documents

Many services are fulfilled digitally through consultation, email, customer portal, messaging, electronic documents, application copies, filing acknowledgements, status updates or other agreed methods. Where a physical document or certificate is required, the applicable delivery method and any related third-party or courier charge will be communicated separately.

Government & Third-Party Decisions

Government departments, regulators, statutory bodies, funding programmes, banks and other third parties independently review and decide applications or requests. Onnmore's professional fee covers the assistance described for the selected service and does not constitute a government fee or guarantee approval, registration, licence issuance, funding or another third-party outcome.

Customer Cooperation

Customers are responsible for providing accurate and complete information, documents and approvals reasonably required for the selected service. Delays caused by missing, inaccurate or late information may affect the service timeline.

Contact

For service delivery questions, contact support@onnmore.com.
