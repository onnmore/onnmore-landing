Who this is for

Anyone starting or running a restaurant, café, cloud kitchen, bakery, catering business, or home-based food business.

When you may need this

Before you start selling food to the public — even a home baker or small cloud kitchen typically needs at least basic FSSAI registration.

Typical documents

Exact requirements vary by case, but these are commonly needed:

  • PAN and Aadhaar of the owner/partners
  • Passport-size photographs
  • Proof of premises (rent agreement/ownership + NOC, if rented)
  • Water testing report, for certain FSSAI license categories
  • List of food products/menu category
  • Business registration proof, if you have one

FAQs

I run a cloud kitchen with no dine-in seating — do I still need FSSAI?

Yes — FSSAI registration/license applies to anyone manufacturing, storing, selling or distributing food, regardless of whether customers dine in.

Can I start selling before my FSSAI is approved?

We'd advise against it — selling food without valid FSSAI registration can attract penalties. It's best to wait for approval or at least have the application filed and acknowledged.

Do I need a separate license for each outlet?

Generally, yes — FSSAI licensing is tied to specific premises, so multiple outlets usually need to be registered individually.
